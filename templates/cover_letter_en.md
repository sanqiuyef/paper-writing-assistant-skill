# Cover Letter Template (English)

> Usage: fill every {{placeholder}}, drop optional paragraphs that do not apply. Keep to one page.
> Source: adapted from scipilot-writing-skill v1.0.0 (MIT License) cover letter playbook.

## Skeleton

Dear Dr. {{Editor Name}},

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "{{Paper Title}}," for consideration as a {{Article Type}} in {{Journal}}.

**Paragraph 1 — what we did + key finding (1–2 sentences)**
Addressing {{research problem}}, this study {{one-sentence method summary}}. {{Key finding with one concrete number}}.

**Paragraph 2 — why this journal (2–3 sentences)**
This work fits the scope of {{Journal}} because {{fit to aims & scope / relevance to the journal's readers / connection to a recent paper in the journal}}. Compared with {{closest prior work}}, the novelty of this study lies in {{1–2 concrete differences, evidence-based, not inflated}}.

**Paragraph 3 — compliance statements (keep the applicable ones)**
This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission and the author order, and declare no competing interests. {{Ethics approval statement if human/animal subjects are involved; data availability statement}}.

**Optional — suggested/opposed reviewers**
We suggest the following reviewers: {{name, affiliation, expertise, reason}}. We ask that {{name}} not be consulted due to {{reason}}.

{{Corresponding author contact details}}

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
{{Corresponding Author}}, on behalf of all authors
{{Affiliation, Date}}

## Common mistakes checklist

- Forgot to change the journal/editor name (fatal — editors notice immediately)
- Copy-pasting the abstract: the letter argues "why this journal should publish it," not "what we did"
- Overclaiming significance ("the most important advance" → state the concrete difference and evidence)
- Missing compliance statements (originality / dual submission / competing interests / ethics)
- Longer than one page
